How the ₹1/Day Membership Works

We believe academic study and reference should be accessible, transparent, and completely free from hidden financial traps.

01

Deducted Daily at 5:00 AM IST

Each day at 5:00 AM IST, exactly ₹1 is deducted from your balance. On days your balance is zero, no debt or penalty ever accumulates.

02

Load Any Amount (₹1 to ₹10,000)

You have full flexibility: load ₹1 for 1 day, ₹30 for a month, ₹365 for a year, or up to ₹10,000 for long-term access.

03

Zero Bank Auto-Debit Mandates

We never lock your bank account into recurring subscription auto-debits. You always decide when and how much to recharge.

04

Cryptographic Verification

All transactions run through Razorpay Payments with anti-tamper confirmation receipts stored in your passbook.
