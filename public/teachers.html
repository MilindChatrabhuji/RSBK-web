About the Faculty Handbooks

The Bloom's Taxonomy handbooks systematically map learning outcomes across cognitive domains (Remembering, Understanding, Applying, Analyzing, Evaluating, Creating). The Miller's Pyramid handbooks outline clinical and practical competency assessment (Knows, Knows How, Shows How, Does) strictly aligned with NCISM curriculum guidelines.
